The people behind the vision

Meet our founders

With a shared commitment to joyful learning, they are building a place where every child can feel encouraged to discover and grow.

Founder

Harshita Tomar

“Every child deserves to learn in an environment where curiosity and confidence grow together.”

With 10 years of teaching experience, my vision is to help every child become confident and independent—not just in the classroom, but in everyday life. Through practical learning, I want children to apply what they learn, trust their abilities, and grow into caring individuals who encourage and support one another.

Founder

Pankaj Pherwani

“A caring school community gives every child the confidence to reach their potential.”

With 25 years of experience in education management, my vision is to share the knowledge and values I have gained in education with the next generation. By giving children a strong foundation and the confidence to explore, we can help them grow, thrive, and shine wherever they are in the world.
